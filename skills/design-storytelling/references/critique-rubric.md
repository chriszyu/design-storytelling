# Design story critique rubric

Use this rubric to locate the highest-impact weakness in an existing story. Scores are optional and should never be presented as an objective measure of quality.

| Dimension | Diagnostic question | Strong signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Is the audience specific, and does the framing reflect what they own, know, and care about? | The story is shaped around the real room rather than a generic title or persona. |
| Point | Is the main takeaway clear as early as the situation allows? | The audience can accurately restate what matters. |
| Stakes | Is the consequence meaningful, specific, and proportionate? | The audience understands why the issue warrants attention now. |
| Causality | Can we follow why the evidence changed understanding and led to a decision? | Activities support a visible chain of cause, insight, choice, and change. |
| Judgment | Are agency, alternatives, constraints, and tradeoffs visible? | The designer's contribution is precise without erasing the team. |
| Evidence | Are claims supported, bounded, and appropriately confident? | Observations, inferences, outcomes, and hypotheses remain distinct. |
| Selection | Does every core element earn its place? | Supporting detail is available without obscuring the main argument. |
| Action | Is the desired understanding, decision, or next step explicit? | The ending follows from the evidence and names what happens next. |

## Critique order

1. Restate the story in one or two sentences.
2. Identify the audience and communication job, or say what is unknown.
3. Name the strongest existing signal.
4. Name the one structural weakness that most limits the story.
5. Diagnose the eight dimensions, focusing detail on the few that matter most.
6. Separate missing facts from writing problems.
7. Recommend the smallest changes with the highest leverage.
8. Rewrite only when useful or requested.

## Common diagnoses

- A clear sentence cannot rescue an unsupported claim.
- A process timeline often hides the decision that made the work matter.
- An outcome that does not answer the original tension creates a false ending.
- Business relevance without a visible mechanism is assertion, not translation.
- Strong craft cannot compensate for inflated authorship or hidden uncertainty.
- A request for “feedback” is not a clear decision or next action.
